Evaluating a claim

The diagnosis and the injury must be connected.

A diagnosis made later than expected does not automatically establish malpractice. A claim needs evidence that the provider departed from the applicable standard of care and that this failure caused injury.

Medical records and the timeline of care help distinguish the effects of the underlying condition from harm associated with a failure to diagnose. That distinction is central to reviewing a potential case.

Arizona law: A.R.S. § 12-563.
